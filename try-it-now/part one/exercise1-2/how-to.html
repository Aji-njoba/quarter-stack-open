how to register fo an examination

this guide explains the steps student should follow to register for an examination successfully.

before you go

check the examination registration dates and make sure you meet the requirements for the examination

what to bring

prepare your registration number, and any required school documents.

At the office

visit the examination office and ask for the registration form. complete the form and submit it to the staff.

complete the form

write your name, student number, course details, and other requested information correctly.

confirm your registration

ask the staff to confirm that your registration has been received and that your details are correct.

After registration

keep your registration confirmation safe and check your examination timetable before the examination day.
