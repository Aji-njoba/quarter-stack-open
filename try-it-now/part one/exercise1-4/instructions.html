generator safety

these instructions are for people using a generator during power outrage.

what you need

  • generator manual
  • suitable fuel
  • safe cables

steps

  1. keep the generator outdoors.
  2. switch it off and let it cool before refueling.
  3. alwaysfollow the manufacturer's instructions

if something goes wrong

if you notice a fuel leak, stop using the generator if safe and get help. never use it indoors because carbon monoxide is dangerous.

COis a harmful gas.
